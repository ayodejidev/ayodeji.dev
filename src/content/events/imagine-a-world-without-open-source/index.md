Presented at Open Source Community Africa's flagship event, this session looks at the infrastructure, learning, and opportunity made possible by open source—and what it takes to sustain it.

## What the session covers

The talk examines how open source supports modern infrastructure, creates practical learning paths, and expands access to technical opportunity. It also considers the responsibility maintainers, companies, and communities share in sustaining the projects they depend on.
