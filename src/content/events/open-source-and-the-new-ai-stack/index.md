The AI stack is moving quickly, but participation is not limited to people training models. This talk maps contributions across models, infrastructure, tooling, applications, documentation, and developer experience.

## Where developers fit

Developers can contribute at every layer of the AI stack: improving models and infrastructure, building tools and applications, creating documentation, and shaping the developer experience around increasingly capable systems.
