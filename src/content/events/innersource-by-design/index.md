This session explores the practices that make internal open collaboration sustainable: clear contribution paths, transparent decision making, discoverable ownership, and incentives that reward work across team boundaries.

## Key ideas

InnerSource succeeds when collaboration is designed into the organization instead of depending on individual heroics. The session connects contribution workflows, ownership, documentation, and incentives into an operating model that teams can adopt incrementally.
